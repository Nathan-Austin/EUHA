About Spicepunk.

Factual snapshot

Spicepunk is an independent chilli sauce maker based in Sursee, Switzerland.

The brand entered EHSA 2026 with two sauces. Peach Riot took Gold and Best in Category in Mild Chili Sauce with a judges' weighted score of 7.53. Red Dynasty took Bronze in the same category at 6.46.

For trade and press enquiries, contact Marcus Pitschke at heat@spicepunk.ch.
